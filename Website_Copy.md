### **Customer Conversion Focus**

* Main problem: Customers want fashionable clothing that fits their style and works for different occasions, but they need an easy way to discover the right collection and decide where to shop.
* Main motivation: Look stylish, feel confident, and find clothing that suits everyday life, work, college, celebrations, and social occasions.
* Desired outcome: Customers visit the store, explore the collections, enquire about products, or place an online order.
